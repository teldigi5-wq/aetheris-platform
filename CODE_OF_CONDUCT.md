# Aetheris Code of Conduct

Aetheris is an engineering project built around evidence, safety, owner control, and respectful technical collaboration. Everyone participating in the repository is expected to help keep discussions useful, professional, and safe.

## Expected behavior

Contributors, reviewers, maintainers, and users should:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, evidence, and engineering decisions;
- explain important trade-offs and challenge unsupported claims constructively;
- protect credentials, personal data, private logs, and security-sensitive information;
- respect the project's explicit safety, governance, and physical-validation truth boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or deliberately disruptive behavior;
- give credit for meaningful technical contributions and avoid misrepresenting other people's work;
- keep bug reports, feature discussions, reviews, and security conversations relevant to the project.

## Engineering integrity

Aetheris deliberately distinguishes between implemented behavior, repository evidence, planned capability, and physical-machine validation. Participants must not knowingly present an unverified capability as verified or bypass safety/governance controls merely to obtain a passing demo.

Security findings must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not publish exploit details, secrets, tokens, private keys, personal data, or sensitive logs in public issues.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include:

- personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, or targeted hostility;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- intentionally introducing secrets, malware, credential theft, destructive automation, or deceptive evidence;
- repeatedly derailing technical discussion after being asked to stop;
- pressuring contributors to weaken safety or verification requirements to make a capability appear complete.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior materially harms project safety or collaboration. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and focused on restoring a productive engineering environment.

If a conduct issue also involves a security vulnerability, sensitive personal information, or credentials, follow the private-reporting guidance in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than posting details publicly.

## Scope

This code applies to participation in the Aetheris repository and project-managed collaboration spaces.
